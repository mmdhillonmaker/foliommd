I work on the side of media that nobody sees on screen: the running order, the coordination, the follow-up call that keeps a piece of content moving. My first real production job was in the programming department at Lahore Rang, and that is still how I think about the work — someone has to hold the pieces together while everyone else does their part.

Since then I have sat in a news election cell during an election, cut and written in an agency creative department, and planned and covered corporate events live. Different rooms, same habit: understand the story, understand the deadline, and make sure the people around you have what they need before they have to ask.

I am comfortable switching between writing, editing and coordinating in the same day, and I would rather solve a problem quietly than let it reach air.
