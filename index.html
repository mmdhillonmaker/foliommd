      Meta Ads (Facebook & Instagram)

      A funnel-based approach to Facebook and Instagram advertising: clean tracking first, structured audiences, systematic creative testing and disciplined scaling.

      1. Goal & funnelPick one campaign objective per funnel stage (Leads, Sales, Traffic or Awareness) and set a target CPL/CPA from the client’s margin, so every ad has a clear success number.
      2. Tracking firstInstall the Meta Pixel and Conversions API, verify the domain, set standard events (Lead, Purchase, Add to Cart, Initiate Checkout) and check deduplication in Events Manager before spending.
      3. Audience architectureCold: broad and interest targeting plus 1–3% lookalikes. Warm: video viewers, page engagers and site visitors (30/90 days). Hot: cart abandoners and form drop-offs. Exclusions stop the layers overlapping.
      4. Creative testingTest 3–5 creatives (static, video, carousel, Reels/Stories 9:16) against different hooks and angles, changing one variable at a time so the result is clear.
      5. Budget & scalingLet ad sets exit the learning phase (~50 optimisation events per week), avoid constant edits, then scale winners in 20–30% steps or duplicate them into fresh audiences.
      6. Review & refreshWeekly check of CPM, CTR, hook rate, frequency and CPA; pause fatigued creatives, refresh with new angles and carry the learnings into the next test.
      Tools
      Meta Ads Manager, Events Manager, Meta Pixel, Conversions API, Business Suite
      Key metrics
      CPL, CPA, ROAS, CTR, CPM, Frequency
      Best for
      Lead generation, e-commerce sales and brand awareness on Facebook & Instagram

      Google Ads (Search & Display)

      High-intent search campaigns built on keyword research, tight structure and smart bidding, with Display used to re-engage visitors.

      1. Keyword researchBuild keyword lists in Keyword Planner and map each to search intent (transactional vs informational), then group them into tightly themed ad groups so ads match what people type.
      2. Account structureSeparate campaigns by product/service and intent; use exact and phrase match for control, broad match only with smart bidding and enough conversion data; keep shared negative keyword lists from day one.
      3. Ads & assetsWrite Responsive Search Ads with 10–15 keyword-led headlines and 4 descriptions, add sitelink, callout, call and lead-form assets, and match the message to a focused landing page.
      4. Bidding strategyStart with Maximize Clicks or manual CPC caps to gather data, move to Maximize Conversions, then target CPA / ROAS once there are roughly 30+ conversions in 30 days.
      5. Display & remarketingUse GA4 audiences, custom intent and in-market segments with responsive display ads, plus placement exclusions and frequency caps to win visitors back without wasting budget.
      6. Weekly optimisationReview search terms to add negatives and new keywords; improve Quality Score (expected CTR, ad relevance, landing page experience); check impression share lost to budget vs rank; adjust by device, location and schedule.
      Tools
      Google Ads, Keyword Planner, Search Terms report, Google Ads Editor, GA4
      Key metrics
      CTR, CPC, Conversion rate, CPA, Quality Score, Impression share
      Best for
      Lead generation, local & service businesses and e-commerce demand capture

      Google Shopping & Performance Max

      E-commerce advertising that starts with a healthy product feed and ends with profit-based bidding — built for Shopify-style online stores.

      1. Feed foundationConnect the Shopify store to Merchant Center (Google & YouTube channel or supplemental feed); write structured titles (brand + product type + key attribute), add GTIN/MPN, accurate price and stock, and high-quality images.
      2. Feed healthClear Merchant Center diagnostics, fix disapprovals, set shipping and return policies, and apply custom labels (bestsellers, margin tiers, seasonal) for campaign control.
      3. Campaign structureRun Standard Shopping with priority levels for query control, or Performance Max segmented by custom labels; keep brand and non-brand traffic separate.
      4. Performance Max setupBuild asset groups by product theme with images, video and headlines, add audience signals (customer lists, in-market) and use new-customer goals so budget is not spent on people who already buy.
      5. Bidding by marginStart with Maximize Conversion Value, then set a target ROAS from the break-even point (1 ÷ profit margin) plus a buffer for profit.
      6. Prune & scaleReview product-level performance, exclude weak products, add account-level negatives, test new creatives and scale budgets gradually on products with proven ROAS.
      Tools
      Google Merchant Center, Google Ads, Shopify, feed rules / supplemental feeds, GA4
      Key metrics
      ROAS, Conversion value, Product-level CTR, Impression share, Feed approval rate
      Best for
      Online stores (Shopify) that want more product visibility and sales

      PPC Campaign Management

      A repeatable system for paid search and paid social: define the numbers, structure the account, test one thing at a time and report in plain language.

      1. Unit economicsWork backwards from margin, lead-to-sale rate and customer value to set a target CPA / ROAS and a realistic budget and test period.
      2. Channel mixUse Search to capture existing demand, Meta / Display to create demand and remarketing to close it; split budget by funnel stage instead of spreading it evenly.
      3. Account structureConsistent campaign naming, clean ad-group themes and UTM conventions, so every click can be traced back to a campaign, ad and keyword.
      4. Testing frameworkHypothesis → one variable (ad copy, landing page, audience or bid strategy) → minimum data threshold → keep, kill or iterate, recorded in a simple test log.
      5. Pacing & reportingDaily spend and anomaly checks, weekly optimisation, monthly report with insights and next actions; shift budget to campaigns beating the target CPA.
      6. Landing page & CROMessage match with the ad, fast load, one clear call-to-action, short forms and trust elements — because conversion rate decides CPA as much as the bid does.
      Tools
      Google Ads, Meta Ads Manager, GA4, Google Sheets, Looker Studio
      Key metrics
      CPA, ROAS, Conversion rate, CPC, Impression share, Lead quality
      Best for
      Businesses that need a predictable flow of leads or sales from paid channels

      Analytics & Tracking

      Tracking is the foundation of every ad account. I plan measurement first, build it with GTM and GA4, and validate it before trusting any report.

      1. Measurement planMap business goals to KPIs and events (lead form, purchase, WhatsApp / call click, add to cart) in a simple tracking document before touching any tags.
      2. Google Tag ManagerSet up the GA4 configuration tag, event tags, triggers and variables (with a data layer where needed) and test everything in Preview mode and Tag Assistant before publishing.
      3. GA4 configurationMark key events, enable enhanced measurement, filter internal traffic, set cross-domain tracking, link GA4 with Google Ads and Search Console, and build audiences for remarketing.
      4. Ad conversion trackingConfigure Google Ads conversions with enhanced conversions and Meta Pixel / Conversions API events, aligned with GA4 so the numbers can be reconciled.
      5. UTM disciplineStandard UTM structure (source / medium / campaign / content) and auto-tagging on Google Ads, so traffic never ends up as “direct” or unattributed.
      6. ReportingLooker Studio dashboards for spend, conversions and revenue by channel, funnel drop-off in GA4 Explore, and a sanity check between GA4 and platform numbers.
      Tools
      GA4, Google Tag Manager, Tag Assistant, Looker Studio, Search Console, Meta Events Manager
      Key metrics
      Conversion rate, Engagement rate, CPA / CAC, Channel attribution, Funnel drop-off
      Best for
      Every ad account — reliable data comes before optimisation

      SEO & Content Support

      Foundational SEO that supports paid campaigns and organic reach: keyword-led pages, clean on-page structure and technical basics.

      1. Keyword & intentUse Keyword Planner, Search Console queries and Google suggestions to pick one primary keyword and intent per page, grouped into simple topic clusters.
      2. On-page SEOTitle tags (~60 characters), meta descriptions (~155), one H1 with a logical H2/H3 structure, clean URL slugs, image alt text, internal links and basic schema markup.
      3. Technical basicsCheck indexing in Search Console, submit sitemap.xml, confirm mobile-friendly layout, canonical tags and page speed (compressed images, lazy loading) with PageSpeed Insights.
      4. Content supportPlan content around keyword clusters and write copy that answers the search intent; on WordPress optimise with Yoast or Rank Math, on Shopify use the product and collection SEO fields.
      5. Paid + organic loopUse search-terms and Search Console data to find keywords worth targeting organically, and use strong organic pages as better landing pages for ads.
      6. Monitor & improveTrack impressions, clicks, average position and organic conversions monthly in Search Console and GA4, then refresh pages that are close to page one.
      Tools
      Google Search Console, Keyword Planner, GA4, PageSpeed Insights, Yoast / Rank Math
      Key metrics
      Impressions, Clicks, CTR, Average position, Organic conversions
      Best for
      Websites and stores that want steady organic traffic alongside ads
